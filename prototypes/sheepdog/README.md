# Sheepdog (prototype)

Real-time flock-herding roguelike. You are the dog. Sheep flee from pressure, so steer the flock through the gate into the pen before time runs out.

- **Move (left thumb)**: a floating stick appears under your thumb anywhere on the left/middle of the screen. It is analog: a **light push is a slow, steady walk** (calm pressure, sheep drift away without bolting), a **full push runs**. Mouse drag works the same way; WASD / arrows too.
- **Bark (right thumb, big button or Space)**: a burst that kicks nearby sheep away and panics them. 2.4 s cooldown, with a short vibration on phones.
- **Calm (hold the button or C)**: stay slow and steady even at full push, e.g. to stare a stubborn sheep forward.
- **Bumping**: the dog is solid against sheep. You shoulder them aside and slow down (rams barely move and stun you). A hard bump while running startles the sheep (panic spreads through the flock, "BUMP!", short vibration); a slow crouching nudge does not. Test: `node tests/sheepdog-bump.mjs`.
- **FULL** button (or F) for fullscreen plus a landscape lock. The field widens to match the screen shape, so there are no black bars on a wide phone.
- Multi-touch tested with `node tests/sheepdog-touch.mjs` (emulated phone: stick, light push, bark with a second finger).
- Sheep have a **flight zone** around the dog (dashed ring). Approach from behind to drive them straight, from the side to turn them. They flock (cohesion, alignment, separation) and graze when left alone.
- **Types**: ewes, rams (ignore light pressure, charge and stun you), panickers (bolt and spread panic), lambs (stick to the flock).
- **Day / night loop**: each **day** you herd the flock into the pen (finish early to earn more **lantern oil**; tap CALL DUSK once 60% are penned). At **dusk** the gate shuts and the **night** begins: darkness with a lantern circle around you, and sheep left outside are exposed.
  - **Foxes** sneak in from the edges, grab lambs and strays and run off with them; bark, chase or bump to make them drop the sheep. With no strays they dig under the fence.
  - **Wolf packs** announce themselves with a howl and an edge arrow, circle the flock, then rush an outer sheep and bite (hp: ewe 2, ram 4, lamb 1) or gnaw the fence. Bark scares them, a crouching stare makes them hesitate, alpha wolves take two barks.
  - Sheep **huddle** when predators are near. The pen fence has hp: hold **CALM** next to a damaged or broken wall to repair it. Hold out until the **dawn** bar fills.
  - Survivors carry over (+1 lamb and +1 sheep each dawn). Lose the whole flock and the run ends. Pick one of three **perks** at every dawn: bark, paws, stare, nose, bell, treats, second dog (chases predators at night), bright lantern, **flares** (button, E), campfire, sturdy fence.
- **Maps**: a map-select screen. Map 1, Home Paddock, is a 5-day campaign: day 5 is a final night (alpha pack, no bear) and surviving it shows a win screen with 1-3 stars (3 = no sheep lost over the whole run). Stars and best score are saved in localStorage; maps 2-5 are listed as coming soon. A NEXT button (top right) skips to the dawn reward pick. Cornered foxes and wolves leave the map and come back later from another edge. Tests: sheepdog-map.mjs, sheepdog-skip.mjs, sheepdog-fox.mjs.
- **Map 2, Hill Pasture** (unlocks after Map 1): 8 days on a world about 2x as wide as the screen, with a following camera and a minimap. The flock starts scattered in a few groups and some sheep hide in rustling long grass, so you first **find** them: walk the hills, BARK to make unseen sheep bleat (yellow arrows at the screen edge and blips on the minimap), walk up to a rustling patch to reveal a hider. Then gather and drive them to the pen far across the map. Days are longer and the nights are one step harder.
- **Sheep acting out** (both maps, more often on Map 2): a **bolter** gets a red ! and makes a run for the edge (get in front of it or bark to head it off; if it leaves the map it is gone for the day but comes home at dawn); a **stuck** sheep (blue ?) is freed by standing still next to it for a couple of seconds; a **wanderer** (Map 2) slips away to hide in long grass. Test: node tests/sheepdog-map2.mjs.
- **Kennel** (button on the map select screen): 7 dogs to unlock with **bones** (earned at the end of every run: stars, days survived), each with its own stats (Kelpie fast, Old English loud bark, Corgi no bump panic, Husky bigger lantern, Terrier keeps foxes and eagles scared, Pyrenees fast fence repair), plus 5 permanent upgrades (bigger flock, stronger fence, more day time, more oil, head-start perk). Pick your dog on the map select screen. Test: node tests/sheepdog-kennel.mjs.
- **Map 3, Wood Edge** (tower defence, 10 days): predator scouts roam by day while you find your sheep; days pay **feed**; at dusk a **build screen** lets you spend it on fence add-ons (thorn hedge, torch, bell, scarecrow, reinforced fence) and post allied dogs (Farm Dog plus any kennel dog you own; more posts open as days pass); at night your dogs defend and a FORM button switches them between holding posts, guarding the gate and chasing. Test: node tests/sheepdog-map3.mjs.
- **Map 3 night (waves)**: the pen now sits in the middle of the world with four dirt lanes (west, north, east, south) leading in. Night comes as 3-6 telegraphed **waves**, each down one lane (a howl, a lane banner and an edge arrow), with breathers between them; clear a wave early and the next comes sooner. The pen has a **FEAR** meter: predators near it frighten the flock, high fear makes the flock push the fence down, and a crouching dog near the pen calms it. Your dogs: **SEND DOG** (tap it, then tap the map) sends the nearest allied dog there for a few seconds; **FORM** switches hold-posts / guard-gate / chase. Add-ons are now thorn, torch, bell and reinforced fence. The final night adds a boss wave with a bear.
- **Night chores (prototype)**: when you pass a day you get a night board of farm jobs picked from the animals in your pen (CHORES_ON flag; a CHORES (testing) button on the home screen opens it directly). Four mini-games: **shearing** (drag the clippers slowly over the fleece; hurry and it misses, rub bare skin and you nick it), **milking** (tap the left/right half to the beat), **egg hunt** (tap the straw to find the glinting eggs, mind the goose), **feeding** (drag scoops of feed from the sack to the hungry troughs). Produce becomes bones; each chore done gives the flock +6 s of day time tomorrow (up to 3). Test: node tests/sheepdog-chores.mjs.
- **DROVE ROAD (new mode, test it from the home screen)**: a Slay-the-Spire style run instead of fixed maps. A branching 12-floor road of encounters: **drives** (the normal day loop on a generated layout that gets bigger and harder with the floor; your whole flock is scattered in it, only the animals that reach the pen survive), **hard drives** (more danger, double coins, a trick to choose), **events** (10 text events that gain or lose livestock, coins or tricks), **markets** (buy animals or a trick, sell for 8), **firesides** (two lambs or a trick) and **the last pasture** (a big boss drive). Goal: reach the end with at least 10 animals (you start with 12; a drive with no losses gives a bonus lamb). Bones are paid at the end. Test: node tests/sheepdog-run.mjs.
- **Sprint and stamina**: a SPRINT button (hold; Shift on keyboard) next to BARK and CALM, and a stamina bar at the bottom. Running costs a little, sprinting a lot (and is about 55% faster); crouching or walking slowly recovers fast, standing still recovers a little. Run dry and you are TIRED: slower, no sprint, until a quarter has come back. Stamina perks: Big Lungs (+25%), Second Wind (faster recovery), Light Paws (cheaper running), Burst (faster sprint). Test: node tests/sheepdog-stamina.mjs.
- **Night encounters (prototype, NIGHT (testing) button on the home screen)**: you stay the top-down dog, in the dark with your lantern. **Lamb Rescue**: five lambs are lost; they bleat now and then (yellow arrows and minimap blips), find them in your lantern light and herd them to the pen; a fox turns up after 28 s. **Fox Hunt**: three foxes sneak in for the flock; scare them off, but a fox that has grabbed a lamb is bold, fast and weaves and ignores you: chase it down (sprint), then BARK or tackle it to make it let go; it escapes with the lamb if it reaches the edge. Test: node tests/sheepdog-nite.mjs.
- **Day raids and pen defenses**: by day some enemy scouts become RAIDERS (an alert, a pulsing red pen and an edge arrow): they gnaw or dig the pen fence and, once it is through, take animals from inside while you are out herding; get back, drive them off and crouch beside the fence to mend it. In DROVE ROAD you spend coins at the market on pen defenses that protect every later pen: reinforced fence, thorn hedge, alarm bell, torches, scarecrow, guard dogs (they chase raiders away) and one-use snares. Test: node tests/sheepdog-raid.mjs.
- **Maps are data** (maps.js, schema documented at the top): world size, pen placement, terrain multipliers, season start, quota range, the mixed herd (weighted animal pool by day) and the enemy list (weighted, by day). Five maps: Home Paddock (5 days), Hill Pasture (8), Wood Edge (10, central pen), River Meadow (12, river with fords, ducks and geese), High Pasture (15, central pen, winter, cows and pigs). Every day on every map is the same loop: find the herd, ward off the day's enemy scouts, pen your quota.
- **Herd animals**: sheep, ram, panicker, lamb, black sheep, goat (needs a stare), **goose** (fast, scatters hard at a bark, loose flock), **duck** (heads for water, swims fast), **pig** (heavy, heads for mud, ignores barks), **cow** (very slow, stays tight with the herd). **Enemies**: fox, **coyote** (faster, wary from further off, picks off loners), wolf pack, eagle, **badger** (tough, two barks to scare, bites hard), bear. Test: node tests/sheepdog-mixed.mjs.
- **Quota**: every day has a minimum number of sheep that must be in the pen when it ends (HUD: PEN x/y, NEED n; it rises across the map, e.g. 50% on day 1 to 80% on the last day on Hill Pasture). Miss it and the run ends (QUOTA MISSED); meet it and the day passes (FINISH DAY appears) with a score bonus for extra penned sheep, and the ones still outside wander home. Test: node tests/sheepdog-quota.mjs.
- **Night is parked** (NIGHT flag in index.html, off by default): each day now ends when everything is penned, the time runs out, or you tap FINISH DAY. Sheep still outside the pen at the end are lost; then you get the reward pick and the next day. The night code (waves, build screen, allied dogs) stays and can be re-enabled while a new night mini-game is designed. Night-only perks are hidden while it is off.
- Keys: Space bark, C calm/stare/repair, E flare, N call dusk, F fullscreen.
- Test: `node tests/sheepdog-bot.mjs 6 --shots dir` plays days and nights with bots (herding by day, predator-chasing and fence-mending by night).
- Serve: `node prototypes/sheepdog/serve.mjs` then http://localhost:8084
